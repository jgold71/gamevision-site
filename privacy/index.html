Privacy Policy for GameVision Soccer Clock

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Developer: Jason Goldsmith
Contact: jgold7.enterprise@gmail.com

This policy describes how GameVision Soccer Clock (“the app”) handles information. The app is a soccer clock and bench tool for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Information the app stores on your devices

GameVision Soccer Clock is designed to work on your iPhone and, if you use one, your paired Apple Watch. It stores the following on the device, so you can run a match:

  • Team names, player names and numbers, and lineup choices you enter
  • Match settings (period length, clock direction, alerts, appearance, and similar options)
  • The live game (clock, score, substitutions, and playing time)
  • History of finished games on that device
  • Your choice to sync a live game between iPhone and Apple Watch

This information stays on your devices. The app does not require an account and does not include a sign-in.

Information we do not collect

The developer does not operate a server that receives your game, team, or device information.

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It does not use your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or motion data for the features described here.

Syncing a live game or your teams between iPhone and Apple Watch uses Apple’s WatchConnectivity, which stays between your paired devices. It is not sent to the developer.

Optional match alerts may use Apple’s local notifications and sounds on your device. Those alerts are scheduled on the device and are not push messages from a developer server.

Apple Watch workout session

To keep the match clock and alerts running on Apple Watch while your wrist is down, the app starts a workout session for the length of a game. The first time you start a game on Apple Watch, the system may ask for permission related to the Apple Health app. GameVision does not read any data from Apple Health, does not write or save a workout (or any other Health data) to Apple Health, and does not use the session to track fitness, steps, or movement. The session ends when you end the game or when the game is archived. If you decline the permission, the clock and alerts may not keep running with your wrist down.

Purchases

If you start a trial, subscribe, or buy the lifetime unlock, payment is processed by Apple through in-app purchase. The developer does not receive your Apple ID password or payment card details. Apple’s handling of payment information is described in Apple’s own privacy policy.

The app may check with the App Store whether your Apple ID has a current trial, subscription, or lifetime unlock, so it can turn features on or off on your iPhone and Apple Watch. That check is performed through Apple’s StoreKit, not through a developer-operated server.

Children

GameVision Soccer Clock is a tool for coaches and other adults running a match. It is not directed at children and is not in the App Store’s Kids category. You may enter names or numbers of youth players for lineup and playing-time purposes. Those details stay on your devices as described above.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete teams, players, and game history in the app.
  • You can stop live sync in Setup so iPhone and Apple Watch each keep their own game.
  • You can delete the app to remove the data stored in it on that device. History is kept separately on iPhone and on Apple Watch; deleting the app on one device does not clear History on the other.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings on your iPhone (Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions), or in your Apple ID account settings.

If you have a question about this policy or want help deleting data you cannot remove in the app, email jgold7.enterprise@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted at this page with a new effective date. The Privacy Policy URL in App Store Connect will point to the current version.
