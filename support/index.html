Frequently asked questions

How do I start and stop the clock?

Start a game on your iPhone or Apple Watch, then use the clock controls to start, pause, and resume the clock. Playing time counts only while the clock is running. When a period ends, start the next one from the same screen.

How do I make substitutions?

Stage the players who are coming on, then wave them in one at a time or all at once. Each change is recorded in the substitution log with the period and the game time.

How do I sync with Apple Watch?

Keep your Apple Watch paired with your iPhone and GameVision installed on both. Turn on live sync in Setup to share one live game between the two. Turn it off if you want iPhone and Apple Watch to each keep their own game. To use a saved team on the watch, set it up on your iPhone first and load it from the watch.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy the subscription or lifetime unlock.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, choose GameVision Soccer Clock, and change or cancel it there. To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple.
